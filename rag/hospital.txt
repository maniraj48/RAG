Sunrise Hospital is located in Hyderabad.

The hospital was founded in 2008.

Dr. Rajesh Kumar is the Chief Medical Officer.

The cardiology department has 25 specialists.

Emergency services are available twenty four hours a day.

The hospital contains 350 patient beds.

The radiology department uses advanced MRI technology.

Patients can book appointments online.

The outpatient department operates from 8 AM to 6 PM.
