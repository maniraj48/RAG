Green Valley University was established in 1995.

The university has five departments.

The Computer Science department offers undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Professor Emily Carter is the head of the Computer Science department.

The university library contains over 100,000 books.

Students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75 percent.

Final examinations are conducted every May and November.

The university campus spans 120 acres.

The annual cultural festival is called Utsav.
