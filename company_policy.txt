Employees are entitled to twenty annual leave days per year.

Unused annual leave can be carried forward for a maximum of five days.

Employees must submit leave requests at least three days in advance.

Office working hours are from 9 AM to 6 PM.

Employees are required to wear identification badges while on company premises.

Remote work is allowed on Fridays.

Internet usage is monitored for security purposes.

New employees undergo a two week onboarding program.

Performance evaluations occur every six months.
